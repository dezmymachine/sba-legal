About Us
SBA Legal Consult is a law firm based in Kumasi, Ghana. We advise local and international clients on matters where law, business and regulation meet. Our practice covers corporate and commercial law, mining, oil and gas, insolvency and restructuring, medical law, foreign direct investment, immigration and nationality, property law, and litigation and ADR. 
Our clients include investors entering Ghana, established local businesses, companies in financial difficulty, healthcare practitioners and institutions, and individuals dealing with land, residence or citizenship. We take the time to understand each client’s objectives and the industry they operate in. We then give advice that is clear, practical and ready to act on.
We also follow legal, economic and regulatory developments in Ghana and abroad. Our clients can rely on advice that reflects the current law and anticipates the changes that may affect them.
The firm is led by its Managing Partner, Dr. Samuel Boadi Adarkwah. He holds a Doctor of the Science of Law (J.S.D.) from Cornell Law School, Ithaca, New York, USA. 

Our Expertise
	Mining, Oil and Gas: licensing, regulatory compliance, local content requirements, and agreements across the extractive sector.
	Corporate and Commercial Law: company formation, corporate governance, commercial contracts, and mergers and acquisitions.
	Insolvency and Restructuring: business rescue, administration, liquidation, and debt restructuring for companies and creditors.
	Foreign Direct Investment: structuring market entry, investment registration, incentives, and protections for foreign investors.
	International Law: cross-border transactions, treaty-related questions, and matters involving more than one legal system.
	Medical Law: regulation of health practitioners and facilities, patients’ rights, consent, and professional liability.
	Immigration and Nationality: work and residence permits, citizenship, dual nationality, and the right of abode for Ghanaians abroad and foreign nationals.
	Property Law: land acquisition, title searches, registration, leases, conveyancing, and land disputes.
	Litigation and ADR: representation in civil and criminal disputes before the courts, and resolution of disputes through arbitration, mediation and negotiation.
